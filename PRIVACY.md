# Meet Standup Turn: privacy policy

Meet Standup Turn runs only on `meet.google.com`.

**What it reads.** The display names in the People panel of the Google Meet call you are in, to work out
the alphabetical speaking order.

**What it stores.** For each meeting link, in your own browser (`chrome.storage.local`):
the names seen in that meeting and who started the most recent standup.

**What it sends.** Nothing. The extension has no server, uses no analytics and does not share or sell any data.
Its only network request downloads the latest version number from the project's GitHub repository
(no data about you or the call is included). Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

Questions: use the "Support" tab of the extension in the Chrome Web Store.
