**Description:**

Does your team do the daily standup in Google Meet in alphabetical order? Meet always lists you first,
so it's hard to see when your turn is.

Meet Standup Turn shows a small chip in the call:
• "You speak after Anna": the person alphabetically before you among the people in the call.
• "Today starts: Péter" (optional): pick it once; on the following days the next person alphabetically starts.
  People who are not in the call are skipped, weekends don't count, and you see your position ("7 of 12").

No setup and no account. Names are read from Meet's People panel and never leave your browser.

## Privacy practices
- **Single purpose:** Shows a Google Meet participant when their turn is in an alphabetical standup, and who starts today.
- **storage justification:** Remembers, per team, who started the last standup, the names seen and the meeting links used, so the next starter can be worked out. Stored locally only.
- **Host permission justification (meet.google.com):** Reads participant names from the People panel of the current call to compute the speaking order and shows a small overlay with the result.
- **Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code.
- **Data usage:** names are processed and stored only in the user's browser; nothing is transmitted.
  Certify: not sold to third parties, not used for unrelated purposes, not used for creditworthiness/lending.
- **Privacy policy URL:** link to PRIVACY.md (must be publicly reachable).
